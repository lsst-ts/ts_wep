Added ``LatissMonolithTask``, a self-contained LATISS wavefront estimation task that runs ``IsrTaskLSST``, ``QuickFrameMeasurementTask``, ``CutOutDonutsScienceSensorTask`` and ``EstimateZernikesDanishTask`` in a single quantum, one per night and detector.

It exists because a LATISS alignment sequence is one bright donut per exposure near the boresight, which the ts_wep detection tasks and ``CalcZernikesTask``'s per-visit clipping are not built for; the Zernike fit itself is the stock Danish subtask.
The one LATISS-specific step before the fit is peak normalization of the stamps: Danish's flux and sky-level parameters are scale dependent, and on raw-ADU LATISS stamps the fit stops after a handful of function evaluations.

The task exposes the Danish reduced chi-square, fitted ``fwhm``, ``nfev`` and ``fit_success`` as output columns, with an optional ``maxChiSquare`` cut, since with a single pair per visit there is nothing to sigma-clip against.
