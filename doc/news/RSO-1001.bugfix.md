`GenerateDonutDirectDetectTask` now returns an empty donut catalog for exposures in which more than `maxSaturatedFraction` (default 0.5) of the pixels are saturated, instead of failing in background subtraction with `No valid points to fit. Variance is likely zero`. Fully saturated in-focus acquisition frames (e.g. taken with the dome lights on) otherwise take the whole visit's `cutOutDonutsCwfsPairTask` / `calcZernikesTask` quanta down with them.
